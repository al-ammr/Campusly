## Brand & Style

This design system blends warm Tuscan agrarian warmth with classical academic prestige, tailored specifically for collegiate social discovery, student living, and campus community life. The aesthetic fuses warm stone backgrounds, tactile sun-baked earth tones, and timeless serif typography with modern progressive web app performance. 

Rather than relying on sterile, generic student portal aesthetics or noisy social feeds, the interface evokes the feel of a curated collegiate journal. It delivers an inviting, grounded, and dignified user experience tailored to students navigating campus organizations, verified notices, housing, and social cohorts. The emotional resonance balances collegiate intellectualism, architectural permanence, and social vitality.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from sunlit limestone, weathered terracotta tile, cypress groves, and antique ink:

- **Primary (`#C85A32`) & Terracotta Deep (`#A8431F`)**: Sunbaked terracotta serves as the central interactive beacon, anchoring primary calls-to-action, key state transitions, and high-priority discovery tags.
- **Secondary (`#2C402E`) & Cypress Light (`#4A634E`)**: Deep botanical olive and cypress represent authority, student governance, campus spaces, and verified academic seals.
- **Tertiary (`#E8B53D`)**: Warm Tuscan ochre provides soft highlights, award accents, and community recognition signifiers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2`) & Stone Surface (`#F3EFEA`)**: Replaces clinical white with tactile parchment tones, eliminating eye fatigue during prolonged late-night study and browsing sessions.
- **Charcoal Text (`#1F1C18` & `#4A453E`)**: High-contrast, deeply legible dark stone inks provide typographic authority without harsh pure-black glare.

## Typography

Typographic scale is structured through a high-contrast editorial pairing:

- **Playfair Display** delivers refined academic authority and literary warmth for section headings, featured community titles, and spotlight cards. It introduces tactile luxury and historical weight uncommon in routine campus apps.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** powers all operational and transactional UI: microcopy, navigational chrome, body passages, tags, and inputs. Its open apertures and contemporary proportions guarantee rapid scan-speeds under high-glare outdoor campus sunlight on mobile displays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first around a fluid structural grid optimized for PWA usage on standard mobile hand-held ratios, scaling smoothly to tablet and desktop:

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins and 16px gutters. Structural navigation collapses into a floating bottom tactile tab bar with safe-area hardware insets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 32px margins and 20px gutters. Dual-pane exploration flows (list view on the left, community detail on the right) engage at this breakpoint.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with max-width content constraints capped at 1280px, flanked by 48px margins and 24px gutters. A fixed editorial side-rail anchors navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic digital drop-shadows. Depth is built through physical parchment layering, subtle tonal surface tiers, and warm umber-tinted ambient glows:

- **Level 0 (Base)**: `#FAF7F2` neutral stone canvas.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Containers / Cards)**: `#F3EFEA` background bounded by a 1px hairline border of `#E3DCD2`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(31, 28, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Surfaces / PWA Overlays)**: `#FAF7F2` surface, border `1px solid rgba(227, 220, 210, 0.8)`, shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(31, 28, 24, 0.08), 0 1px 4px rgba(31, 28, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Bottom Trays)**: Background `#FAF7F2`, shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(31, 28, 24, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces tailored craftsmanship:
- Base inputs, buttons, and cards utilize moderate radii (`0.5rem` / 8px) to retain structural discipline and architectural form.
- Elevated discovery cards and student showcase modules employ medium architectural curves (`1rem` / 16px).
- Badges, pill filters, verified emblems, and bottom navigation handles transition into full capsule rounds (`2rem` / 32px).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Terracotta background (`#C85A32`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` border-radius, font weight 600. Active states shift to Terracotta Deep (`#A8431F`).
- **Secondary / Academic**: Cypress background (`#2C402E`), parchment text (`#FAF7F2`), subtle hover lift.
- **Outline / Editorial**: Transparent fill, 1px border (`#E3DCD2`), ink charcoal text (`#1F1C18`). On tap, surface tints to `#F3EFEA`.

### Cards & Editorial Containers
- **Community Discovery Cards**: Encased in Stone Surface (`#F3EFEA`) with a crisp 1px `#E3DCD2` border and `1rem` radius. Header titles are rendered in Playfair Display with supporting operational metadata (faculty, member count, meeting frequency) set in Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Featured Campus Notice**: Terracotta hairline top accent strip (3px `#C85A32`) over a warm parchment card, highlighting high-priority announcements.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Verified Student / Faculty Badge**: Cypress green fill (`#2C402E`) or Verified green pill (`#2B6E3F`) with white lettering, accompanied by an enclosed check glyph, styled at `11px` bold uppercase (`label-sm`).
- **Community Tag**: Warm ochre tint (`#FAF7F2` background with `#E8B53D` border and `#1F1C18` text) or Stone Surface pill (`#F3EFEA`) with subtle border.

### Input Fields
- Structured with `#FAF7F2` fill, 1px `#E3DCD2` border, `0.5rem` corner radius, and Plus Jakarta Sans text.
- Focus states transition the border smoothly to terracotta (`#C85A32`) paired with a 2px diffuse ring in `rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.15)`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Distinctive square-rounded (4px) check boxes and circular radio rings bordered in `#4A453E`.
- Selected state fills with Terracotta (`#C85A32`) for active community subscriptions or Cypress Green (`#2C402E`) for academic credential filters.

### Mobile Navigation Dock (PWA)
- Persistent frosted warm bar docked at the mobile base: `#FAF7F2` with 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, top hairline border of `#E3DCD2`.
- Icons use Cypress Green (`#2C402E`) with active indicators shifting to Terracotta (`#C85A32`) alongside an ochre micro-dot indicator.
